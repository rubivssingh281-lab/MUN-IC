ABOUT SAARTHI 2026

"Diplomacy, Dialogue, and Dharma"

SAARTHI 2026 is a premier model united nations conference that aims to encapsulate the essence of modern diplomacy enriched with timeless wisdom. It provides an platform for delegates to blend international diplomacy with dharmic values, fostering a spirit of mutual respect and constructive debate.

Our Mission

To cultivate a generation of leaders equipped with diplomatic skills and a profound sense of responsibility towards global issues.

Who We Are

We are a dedicated team of passionate individuals committed to providing an exceptional MUN experience that challenges and inspires.

The Theme

Exploring the intersection of modern statecraft and eternal principles, guiding delegates to seek righteous resolutions.
