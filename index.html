ABOUT SAARTHI 2026

"Diplomacy, Dialogue, and Dharma"

"Through diplomacy, debate, negotiation, and critical thinking, SAARTHI invites delegates to step beyond the comfort of silence and into the responsibility of leadership. Every argument becomes an opportunity to question, every disagreement a chance to understand, and every resolution a step towards meaningful change."

Our Vision

To cultivate a generation of leaders equipped with diplomatic acumen, critical thinking, and a profound sense of responsibility towards global dilemmas.

Who We Are

The Theme

Exploring the intersection of modern statecraft and eternal dharmic principles, guiding delegates to seek righteous, sustainable global resolutions.
