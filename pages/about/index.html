ABOUT SAARTHI 2026

Diplomacy, Dialogue, and Dharma — Bridging Timeless Wisdom with Modern Multilateral Statecraft.

"Through diplomacy, debate, negotiation, and critical thinking, SAARTHI invites delegates to step beyond the comfort of silence and into the responsibility of leadership. Every argument becomes an opportunity to question, every disagreement a chance to understand, and every resolution a step towards meaningful change."

Our Vision

To cultivate a generation of leaders equipped with diplomatic acumen, critical thinking, and a profound sense of responsibility towards global dilemmas.

Who We Are

We are a dedicated team of passionate diplomats and thinkers committed to providing an exceptional MUN experience that challenges preconceptions and inspires action.

The Theme

Exploring the intersection of modern statecraft and eternal dharmic principles, guiding delegates to seek righteous, sustainable global resolutions.

THE THREE PILLARS

The philosophical foundation of SAARTHI Model United Nations

01

Diplomacy (राजनय)

Cultivating high-stakes multilateral problem-solving, strategic consensus-building, and international cooperation across sovereign boundaries.

02

Dialogue (संवाद)

Encouraging respectful, evidence-based discourse where divergent ideological perspectives converge to craft actionable, forward-thinking pacts.

03

Dharma (धर्म)

Grounding modern policy solutions in ethical righteousness, equity, sustainability, and moral accountability towards future generations.
