FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Essential information for prospective delegates, faculty advisors, and applicants.

What is the portfolio allotment procedure?

Country and portfolio allocations are processed on a first-come, first-served basis following registration verification and past MUN experience assessment.

Are beginner delegates supported?

Yes, the World Health Organization (WHO) committee is specifically designated as beginner-friendly, featuring pre-conference webinars, dais mentorship, and guided Rules of Procedure (ROP).

How can institutions register a group delegation?

Use the School Delegation Form track to register contingents. An assigned Secretariat liaison coordinates portfolio requests directly with your institution's faculty advisor.
